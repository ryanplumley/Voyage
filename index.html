How to use this

This is a private little helper for stateroom gift exchanges on your cruise. It turns the organizer’s list into something you can sort, search, and tick off door to door.

Your lists never leave your phone. Files are read right here on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server — not to this site or anywhere else. Even when you load a Google Sheets link, the sheet is fetched directly by your device and the link itself is only saved on your device.

Loading your lists

1
Tap Choose file(s) (or Load / add a file in the ⋯ menu) and pick what your organizer shared.
2
It accepts .pdf, .xlsx, and .csv. You can load several at once, or add more later — each file just gets added in.
3
One file can hold more than one exchange (the Fish Extender workbook has Family, Kids, and Adult lists), and they’ll all show up.

The exchanges

Pixie Dust — one big list of staterooms, with families’ favorites and occasions.
Fish Extender (FE) — split into groups (Mickey, Minnie…). Family, Kids-only, and Adult-only versions.
Magnet & Ornament — simple lists split into groups, just last name + stateroom.

Picking your exchange & group

1
Use the top exchange dropdown to switch between the lists you loaded.
2
When you load a list that’s split into groups, you’ll be asked which groups you’re in. The app then shows just those — no clutter from the rest. You can change this anytime with Choose my groups in the ⋯ menu.
3
The group dropdown next to the exchange lets you flip between All my groups, any single group you picked, or Everyone (all groups) when you need to see the whole list.
4
Sort by group, deck, last name, room, or occasion. The deck dropdown narrows to one floor. Search matches names, rooms, and characters.

Checking off deliveries

Tap the circle on a card to mark a stateroom delivered — it dims and the progress ring fills. Tap again to undo. Check-offs are tracked separately for each exchange and saved on this device. Filter to To do or Delivered anytime.

Cards flag occasions (birthdays, anniversaries…) with a ribbon, and allergies / dietary notes in a pink callout, so nothing slips by.

Keep it on your phone (works offline)

Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app and works with no signal — perfect for at sea.

Add to your Home Screen first, then load your lists in the installed app. The Home Screen version is a separate copy — lists and check-offs you added in the browser won’t carry over to it, so it starts empty. Do the install before you load anything to avoid re-doing it.
iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (the box with an up-arrow), scroll down, then tap Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen (sometimes shown as Install app).

Do this while you still have wifi. After the first open it keeps working offline, and your lists and check-offs stay put.

Managing lists

The ⋯ menu lets you add more files, reset check-offs for the current exchange, remove one exchange, or remove everything. When a newer list comes out, just load the new file — it replaces the old one with the same name and keeps your check-offs where the families match.

Loading from a Google Sheets link

If your organizer shares a Google Sheets link instead of a file, you can load it directly:

1
In Google Sheets, tap Share and set access to “Anyone with the link” → Viewer. Then copy the link.
2
In this app, choose Load from Google Sheets link (on the welcome screen or the ⋯ menu), paste the link, and give it a name.
3
The sheet is downloaded once and saved on your device — after that it works offline like any other loaded list.
Loaded a list this way? Use Refresh this list from Google Sheets in the ⋯ menu (while online) to pull the latest updates without losing your check-offs.
The link only goes from your device straight to Google — it’s saved on your device only, never sent anywhere else.

Load from Google Sheets

Paste a link to a Google Sheet that’s shared as “Anyone with the link” → Viewer. If a specific tab is open in your browser, that tab will be loaded.

Which groups are you in?

Pick the groups you’re delivering to. You’ll see just those — and can still switch to All my groups or any single group. Change this anytime from the ⋯ menu.
